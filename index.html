Controls and explanation

Click or tap the canvas to add a green landmark (POI); click near one to remove it. Add at least three well-spaced landmarks to help the pink particles find the blue robot, Tupac.

Focus the canvas for keyboard controls: Up/Down = sensing noise; Right/Left = movement noise; W/S = ray count; Shift = turning noise; V = experimental ray-sensing mode. Lower mean error is better.

The square world wraps at its edges. Landmark sensing measures direct distance to each known POI; ray mode measures the nearest visible square, up to 200 pixels. No landmarks means no measurement update, not a successful localization.
