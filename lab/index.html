What am I looking at?

01 / Predict. Each particle is a possible position and heading. Noisy odometry moves every hypothesis through the same collision-aware map.

02 / Observe. Ray-cast lidar and UWB ranges score those hypotheses. The filter never receives the robot's true position. Ground truth is shown only for evaluation.

03 / Refine. Log-space weights avoid numerical collapse. Low effective sample size triggers systematic resampling; poor sensor agreement introduces global recovery proposals.

A known-map simulation, not SLAM or production navigation. Noise is idealized Gaussian noise. The ellipse is a Gaussian approximation around the dominant position hypothesis, not a calibrated confidence guarantee. Lidar alone can be ambiguous; UWB does not directly observe heading.
